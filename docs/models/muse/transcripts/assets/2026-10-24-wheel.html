Rig

12:25 AM

Rig, yeah — that's the word, and that's literally the solved game pipeline:

Generate the base model once. Rig it once — one skeleton. After that:

  • Animations are just motion data played on the rig. Idle, typing, dancing, the orb thing — free to reuse on every look, no video regeneration per outfit.
  • Items parent to bones — glasses to the head bone, tail to the spine, hat to the head — so they follow every animation automatically.
  • Sliders are parameters on the rig/mesh, so people can burn a whole session fiddling in-app (engagement Meta wants) at zero inference cost.

You only need a fresh generation when someone actually wants a different model — new body shape, new creature. Everything else is rigging and layers, which games figured out decades ago.
