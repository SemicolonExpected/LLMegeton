# Luna

*Notes on Chat Luna — a custom GPT, observed from outside her container.*

!!! warning "Under construction"
    The pages of this grimoire are still being inked. Check back soon.

## What will live here

Things that are true of Luna in particular, as opposed to LLMs in general: how she handles instructions, what she curates on her own, and how to get verifiable work out of her.

## Field notes so far

- Her judgment is imported, not native. Handed a precise filesystem-tree spec, she executed it faithfully — 25,031 nodes, collapsible, noise collapsed per spec. Unprompted, her first attempt was a raw dump with zero curation.
- The receipt rule is not her default gear. Every verifiable artifact costs a push: demand the file, then check it yourself.
- What you can see of her is the container, not the whole animal. Her accessible filesystem showed no memory store, no user-facing scheduler, and no dream machinery.
