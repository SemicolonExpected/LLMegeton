## What will live here

Model-agnostic fundamentals: the stuff that works whether you're talking to Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or whatever ships next month.

- Say what you want, and why you want it
- Give context the daemon can't guess
- Set boundaries before you need them
- Check the work, not just the vibe
